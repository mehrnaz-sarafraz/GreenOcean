# Release readiness checklist

Code-side preparation exists; these items require product, legal, design, infrastructure or store accounts and are intentionally not executed yet.

- Confirm legal entity, privacy/terms URLs, support contact and account/data deletion process.
- Commission trademark review, final store screenshots/copy and age/content ratings.
- Review Persian and English copy with native-language and mental-health safety specialists.
- Run screen-reader, keyboard, dynamic-text, contrast, reduced-motion and RTL acceptance on physical devices.
- Configure Apple/Google signing, bundle ownership, EAS credentials and store privacy/data-safety forms.
- Complete penetration testing, dependency remediation, abuse testing and credential-document access review.
- Provision TLS, secret manager, managed PostgreSQL, encrypted media/object storage, monitoring and off-site backups.
- Execute restore, rollback, migration and incident-response drills.
- Verify crisis-resource copy and geographic coverage with a qualified safety owner.
