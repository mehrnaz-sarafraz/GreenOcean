Required evidence: controller/contact and accountable roles; intended jurisdictions/operator classification; purpose-specific lawful bases and sensitive-data conditions; approved documents/translations; processor/transfer arrangements; retention/holds/backup replay; real rights/report/incident staffing; native/assistive and production access/load review.
