# Processing purposes and legal review

This is a technical decision aid, not selected lawful bases, legal advice or GDPR/DSA compliance. The earlier decision register records Iran as a planning market only. Operator establishment, controller identity/contact, target users, hosting, licenses and service territories must be confirmed by owner and qualified local counsel. No territory is enabled here. Iran-specific consumer/health/privacy requirements remain LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED; no statutory deadline is guessed.

## Authoritative references and limits

Reviewed 2026-10-27: [EDPB territorial scope](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3-version_en) explains establishment/targeting; accessibility worldwide alone is not treated here as a scope determination. [Commission legal-grounds guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/legal-grounds-processing-data_en) informs the separation of affirmative purpose-specific consent/withdrawal and special-category review. [Commission individual-rights information](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en) informs the distinction between selected self-service export and broader access review.

The [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) and [DSA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj) require professional application to the actual operator. EUR-Lex full text returned an automated-browser challenge during this continuation; it is not represented as freshly read in full. For DSA, assess actual intermediary/hosting/platform classification, territorial scope, organizational size, exceptions and affected decisions before choosing duties. No VLOP classification, DPO, approved DPIA, supervisory approval, transfer safeguard or signed contract is asserted. A staff reference or environment flag is not legal authorization.
