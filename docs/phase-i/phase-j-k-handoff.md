## J — decisions requiring an owner and qualified operational/legal review

The owner-approved model stays live text after explicit real acceptance, no queue. Do not introduce voice/video, sensitive topic matching, diagnoses, risk scoring, third-party trackers, expanded countries, historical message widening or new clinical claims under this change.

Unresolved launch decisions: full approved listener code and current-version review, independent practice evidence and real staff/supervisor coverage; communication of the bounded activation/reconnection defaults; lawful request/session/message processing and notices; metadata/history/report evidence retention and access, backup/deletion limits; real geographic availability and incident escalation responsibilities. No GDPR/legal/clinical compliance declaration is made. Preserve received histories under existing rules; a new retention/destruction policy requires approval rather than inference. Aggregate operational metrics remain disabled until purpose/access/retention are approved. Safe interim behavior is the default-off live service with real non-listener and public urgent-resource choices.
