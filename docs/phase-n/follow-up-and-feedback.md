# Voluntary follow-up
After an authorized completed N session, its member may skip feedback or explicitly save any nonempty subset of helpful/respected/would-request-again booleans. No narrative, transcript, journal or diagnosis is collected. Answers are not a clinical outcome score and never feed matching, advertising, listener ranking or automatic misconduct reports.

Owner-only GET/PUT/DELETE and revision checks protect feedback. Exact retries are idempotent; changed stale drafts conflict rather than overwrite another device. Editing stops thirty days after session completion, and edits never renew the retention deadline. A separate existing report/block route remains available. No automatic follow-up contact is sent.

The recent-session dashboard shows only the caller's participation and their own feedback-saved state. A listener cannot learn the member's saved answers or feedback state from that endpoint. Existing I history and message permissions remain authoritative.

Repeat booking is independently optional, even when feedback is skipped. The server rechecks current account/region qualification, blocks, calendars and capacity. A returned actual slot filters the ordinary future-booking workflow; it does not itself reserve anything. A new booking/request/conversation never inherits a prior transcript or permanent direct-message rights. Private durable notes remain disabled because their sensitive purpose/access policy was not approved.
