# Booking cancellation, grace and no-show
Cancellation is penalty-free and participant-owned. A pending child I offer is cancelled atomically with its booking. Active sessions use I end controls. Completed/cancelled retries return their terminal outcome. Future confirmed rescheduling preserves the old reservation if replacement cannot commit; stale keys never revive a cancelled slot.

Check-in opens five minutes before the appointment and the grace period ends ten minutes after its start. Only the owning member checks in. At/after the start they must explicitly request a genuinely ready listener, followed by real I acceptance and member activation. A request admitted within the grace window follows I's independent short deadlines; every later acceptance/activation still protects next commitments.

No check-in by grace end: MEMBER_NO_SHOW. Checked in but readiness never confirmed: LISTENER_NO_SHOW, described as unconfirmed listener availability rather than a clinical or moral judgment. Checked in with a ready listener but no completed handoff: EXPIRED / HANDOFF_NOT_COMPLETED. Authorization, regional or block changes: CANCELLED_BY_SERVICE. A declined/expired linked offer keeps its actual I reason instead of inventing a no-show.

A delayed start must still fit a full thirty-minute plan and break before another commitment. Otherwise the member receives a capacity conflict and can cancel and choose another real slot. Sessions are not silently shortened, reassigned or made recurring. No fees, punitive scoring or inferred crisis action is introduced.
