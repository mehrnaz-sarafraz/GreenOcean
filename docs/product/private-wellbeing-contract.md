# Private wellbeing contract
My Wellbeing unifies owner-only journal, mood, real goal steps, optional daily questions and fixed-prompt guided writing. Participation is optional. Explicitly saved writing stays private; there is no automatic publication or reward for disclosure. No diagnosis, psychological risk estimate, therapist simulation, AI transfer or continuous monitoring is introduced. Urgent resources stay independent. Historical assessment data is preserved, but the numerical total is not presented as a validated measure or treatment recommendation. O1 privacy/lifecycle/authorization and retention details are authoritative in ../phase-o/.
