# Schedules and readiness
Private DRAFT intervals do not advertise clinical availability. Publishing requires current credential, exact modality/country, eligibility and six separate platform evidence purposes. Intervals use UTC instants and an explicit IANA timezone. Weekly rules are bounded to one year and materialize a rolling 30-day window. Nonexistent DST times are skipped; ambiguous times require REJECT/EARLIER/LATER policy. Existing drafts can be explicitly published; exact interval retries reuse identity. PostgreSQL exclusion constraints and a shared professional scheduling advisory lock prevent capacity overlap. Cancelled slots can be legitimately recreated. Exceptions and stopping recurrence preserve reservations for explicit subsequent handling.

An authenticated participant can explicitly check in near a confirmed appointment. Readiness expires in 60 seconds and also requires a current unrevoked authentication session and current care authorization. A scheduled slot, delivered notification or readiness flag is not proof of delivered consultation or a professional no-show. Reminder preference is per participant, opt-in, revocable. Bounded configurable defaults are24h/60m/15m/start; the default-off worker creates generic in-app notices only within a two-minute due window and respects notification/quiet-hour restrictions. No native push delivery or device ringing is claimed.
