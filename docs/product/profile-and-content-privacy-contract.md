# Profile and content privacy contract

G/H extends A–F; account eligibility and session policy still apply before social access.

Account identity (email, eligibility country, age assurance, policy evidence) is private. Social identity is a random, editable username and display name; duplicate display names are valid. Private profiles remain discoverable to eligible members by minimal identity including an optional public avatar. Avatar selection therefore discloses that image to eligible members; it is not private media. Bio and explicitly enabled social location/year are visible only to self, public-profile viewers, or approved followers. New users are not prompted for age. Counts must not reveal anonymous or inaccessible posts. Support preferences never appear in social DTOs.

Post audience is durable and independent of profile privacy: FOLLOWERS means approved current followers; COMMUNITY means current members of the selected community; PUBLIC means eligible authenticated members, never Internet guests. Owner access is retained. Blocks override all ordinary member access. Follow approval never grants community access. Moderation/deletion overrides visibility. All read/action endpoints use the same database audience predicate. A private profile may explicitly publish a member-wide post; the composer requires a separate informed confirmation. Changing profile privacy does not change any existing post audience.

Historical PUBLIC rows have no reliable per-post informed-consent evidence. Migration conservatively converts **all pre-G/H PUBLIC rows to FOLLOWERS**, including currently public profiles: current visibility cannot reconstruct original intent. This reduces discoverability, never increases it. Rows are retained; privacy choices, follows and communities are unchanged. New PUBLIC publication requires explicit confirmation. No automated audience widening or rollback to old audience logic is safe.

Anonymous author identity is omitted from post/comment DTOs and excluded from other members' author-profile listings and counts. Notification actors for anonymous comments remain absent; anonymous comment likes do not expose the liker as that comment's author. Anonymity hides attribution from ordinary members, not platform safety/authorized legal processes. Do not reuse anonymous author IDs in recommendations.

Message requests off prevents unsolicited starts; a target's approved follow of the sender permits contact. Established consensual conversations remain separate, but blocks prevent listing, reading, starting and sending. Professional care and user-initiated listener matching retain separate service authorization; neither requires enabling unsolicited messages globally. Community membership is separate from follows.

Blur is a visual presentation preference, not encryption: authorized clients receive permitted content. Stronger controls exclude warning-marked content from discovery; medication reduction and muted terms also exclude matching discovery results. Direct authorized post links retain a warning/reveal boundary and mandatory moderation. Notifications never include social post body previews. Previously received content cannot be remotely erased; active views revalidate on focus and clear failures. No claim of clinical confidentiality.

Export includes owner profile, private preferences and onboarding progress through the existing account archive. Deletion uses existing erasure/session invalidation and removes preferences/progress. Avatar URLs reference externally hosted images: GreenOcean stores no uploaded avatar binary and cannot erase third-party copies; URL removal erases the reference. Backups/security exceptions follow existing retention documentation. No automatic health inference or treatment promise.
