# For Further Reading contract
The section is titled For Further Reading / برای مطالعه بیشتر. It offers general education, not a personal assessment, diagnosis or unsolicited professional consultation. Original post authors explicitly opt in per post and may revoke or remove associations. Existing anonymous authorship and every audience/block/group/moderation restriction remain authoritative; editors acquire no exceptional narrative access.
Proposals and independent current-scope review bind to exact post, preference and reviewed-resource versions. Retirement, revocation or content/access changes remove active recommendation. No private narrative or group membership is placed in educational search, notifications or external AI systems. See ../phase-o/resource-attachment-privacy.md for states, locks and DTO boundaries.
