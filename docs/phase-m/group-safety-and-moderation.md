# Group safety, reports and independent appeals

Global Phase L standards and decisions remain authoritative. Group rules may narrow acceptable behavior, never authorize prohibited content. Local screening has documented semantic limitations; human capacity and policy qualification remain release gates. No group moderator obtains a platform role, private-message access or anonymous attribution.

Reports reuse reports, the existing owner reports UI and moderation_appeals. COMMUNITY, POST, COMMENT, USER and EVENT targets are bound to their actual group, including reports submitted through the legacy reporting API. Member-scoped submission validates current access; guessed hidden group/event IDs return nondisclosing404. Global report budgets and same-reporter/target locks prevent duplicate open reports. Owner/moderator misconduct and group-governance reports go to independent platform review, not the reported group manager.

The group report queue contains current permitted item bodies/revisions and policy reasons, with no reporter identity, author mapping or unfiltered narrative report description. Managers cannot resolve their own reports or hide their own content. Current manager permission, ACTIVE status, exact revision and row locks are checked before a scoped hide. This creates a separate community_content_actions restriction without changing the global status, author, anonymity or audience. Ordinary feeds, direct reads, bookmarks, comment actions/counts and notifications exclude the restricted revision. Changed bodies still undergo Phase L screening; a prior decision does not silently approve a new revision.

The content owner can see their group action, submit one bounded appeal and retain owner export/deletion rights even after removal. Independent current platform reviewers with recent strong MFA review purpose-audited evidence. Original actor/appellant cannot decide the appeal. Removing a group restriction changes only that restriction; globally blocked content remains blocked. Generic legacy appeal endpoints cannot override this workflow. Reports without a group hide retain the established report appeal pathway; member sanctions can be challenged through the existing owner appeal/safety process rather than an automatic unban.

Platform reports are reviewed separately with a decision note. Resolving a report records a human disposition; it does not pretend to execute a hidden treatment, suspension or global publication decision. Entire-group lifecycle actions are separately authorized/audited. Reports/appeals stay pending when actual staff are unavailable. New generic notifications carry no hidden group title, member list, body text or invitation secret; detailed reads always recheck current authorization.

Platform purpose queues are bounded. Ordinary dashboard group lists and report/appeal projections exclude hidden group evidence and group action appeals. Existing ADMIN-only L attribution remains the only justified anonymous identity path. No group manager has that capability.
