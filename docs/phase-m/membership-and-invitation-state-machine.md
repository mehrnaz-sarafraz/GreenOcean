# Membership and invitations

All membership, invitation, role and transfer mutations lock the group row before checking current eligibility/roles/status. This serializes cross-instance admission, cancellation, removal and role changes. Public admission requires current rules acknowledgment; PRIVATE requests bind their own monotonic request revision and rules version. Cancellation/approval races have one committed outcome; an older request approval cannot approve a newly submitted request. Declined requests have a bounded one-hour retry pause, not an automatic account sanction.

HIDDEN never uses direct self-join. Managers issue target-bound in-app invitations with UUID retry keys, default7day configurable expiry (1..30days),20 pending/group and30 issued/hour/actor. Opening the inbox grants no membership. Recipient sees minimal approved name/type/current rules; explicit acceptance revalidates target, inviter's current manager permission, blocks, bans, group status, expiry and rules version. No reusable secret, public invite link, email narrative or recipient roster is introduced. ACCEPTED retry is idempotent only while membership still exists; it cannot resurrect a removed member. Revocation after acceptance conflicts and directs the manager to member management.

REMOVE and BAN are distinct. Removal revokes current membership/attendance/invitations/transfers. BAN additionally persists an exclusion with reason/actor/optional expiry; rejoin cannot bypass it. Lifting a ban never silently recreates membership. Temporary restrictions are explicit and current rules are required before narrative contributions. Membership action history is owner-exportable without staff attribution.

Transfer offers require the current sole owner and an eligible current member. The target explicitly accepts or declines; owner may cancel. Acceptance atomically demotes the prior owner and promotes the target, with an audit event. Stale/expired/cancelled offers cannot escalate roles. Account erasure archives groups actually owned at deletion time, not groups merely created historically by a former owner. Owner-unavailable recovery remains a separately privileged reviewed procedure, never a member self-promotion route.
