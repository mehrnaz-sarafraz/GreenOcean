# Bounded rules and limitations

Reuse L ContentSafety local-screen-v1 and existing auth/report transaction limits; no new psychological classifier or third-party narrative processor. Fixed link/contact/script patterns may hold public submissions; defined threats/abuse/promotions have bounded existing outcomes and current-revision human review. Private journals, reflections, clinical notes and listener transcripts are not added to screening.

Q1 regressions include sadness, anger, grief, loneliness, disagreement and Persian/English/mixed text as legitimate expressions. Unicode normalization applies to a matching copy, never the stored narrative. Quotes/context/obfuscation are imperfect: false positives and negatives remain possible. Keyword tests do not establish semantic understanding or clinical risk diagnosis. Consequential review stays in authorized human source workflows. Coordination imports and transitions use cross-instance database budgets and revision checks.
