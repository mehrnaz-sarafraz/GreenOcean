# Controlled pilot operating plan

Goal: assess safe operation and usability for an explicitly authorized100–500 registered adult cohort; start with capacity100, not automatic first-arrival enrollment. Measured Q4 target50–100 concurrent synthetic clients is localhost evidence, not a production guarantee. No therapeutic benefit claim or clinical-outcome experiment is authorized.

Before invitations: owner names exact immutable source/CI artifact, service/country/cohort/window; independently review all13 criteria, remaining dependency advisories, privacy/processing and legal authorizations, current staff/primary+escalation acknowledgements, approved notice, provider and key custody, staging/production recovery, capacity budget, physical-device package and rollback plan. Record responsible roles, actual opaque evidence references and expiry; never invent names, licenses or contacts. Expected operating hours remain **unapproved**; closed services must show unavailable rather than promise immediate help.

Owner configures service flags separately; GO_RECOMMENDED/OWNER_APPROVED never toggles runtime. Trial one approved stage/service at a time. Invite named eligible accounts within cap, let each review the versioned optional notice, accept independently or decline. Explain peer support versus professional treatment, availability, account/privacy rights, support/feedback and report-abuse routes. Existing support page and pilot center expose support/privacy; service report controls remain available. No hidden marketing or new sensitive satisfaction collection.

During window: staff watches Q3 quality/incident queues and acknowledged primary/escalation delivery, Q4 pool/query/worker/storage signals, booking/payment/refund invariants, support demand, opt-outs and current role/coverage expiry. Monitoring lacks an approved real destination until separately configured. Follow existing incident response and communications templates, without exposing sensitive incident details.

On stop: pause affected new admissions through Q3 containment and service runtime flags; revoke affected assessment/invitations. Preserve current-care handoff, financial reconciliation, protected records and account rights. Do not drop database schemas or roll back applied migration SQL. Verify isolation, communicate accurate scope/next update through independently approved communications, recover forward and repeat affected regression/review before any owner-authorized restart.

Success criteria: no confidentiality or authorization breach; consistent booking/ledger/reward invariants; supported current coverage; bounded errors/latency on approved infrastructure; truthful feedback/support resolution and account rights. Post-pilot independent review assesses minimal aggregate operational data, incidents and blockers, then owner decides stop/change/continue; no automatic expansion.
