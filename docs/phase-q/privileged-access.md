# Privileged evidence access

Every privileged operation revalidates the active verified account, live session, token version, current role and recent strong authentication. Q2 uses the existing SessionPolicy interval for Q1 coverage instead of a separate hardcoded freshness duration. Removal of any role atomically revokes that user's sessions, pending/approved exceptional grants and trust coverage; regranting a role cannot revive an old session. Active evidence reads lock participating users before source/request/session rows, serializing with revocation. Four real independent-connection Q2 races passed, including role removal versus reveal.

Social attribution: source must be currently held/blocked or have an open report; purpose is one of THREAT_INVESTIGATION, PRIVACY_COMPLAINT, ACCOUNT_SECURITY. Requester and reviewer must be independent of each other and the source author. Requests bind source revision and requester's SID/token version. The reviewer approves that exact revision using their own current session. Expiry is at most fifteen minutes, request budget ten/hour, queue fifty. Reveal rechecks both identities, same source revision and requester's original session and consumes the grant atomically once. Returned queue rows contain no author identity or narrative copy. Historical single-admin purpose-only calls fail closed. The UI clears revealed identities on background, navigation, rejected revalidation or sixty seconds; stale asynchronous responses cannot restore them.

Clinical exceptional access extends existing ClinicalCare rather than creating a second medical record service. The two records operators must be independent of the patient and treating professional; ordinary source participants keep their source rights. Exact revision, current category-policy identifier, requester/reviewer session and token version, recent MFA, expiry and revocation are checked at access time. Withdrawal revokes both the request and its grants. V86 deliberately leaves historical session bindings NULL; historical grants cannot become usable through guessed backfilling. Source role and policy changes fail closed. Raw session identifiers are not returned by the staff queue.

Fixed-purpose audit records trace actual actors and decisions. No staff, MFA credential, shift, approval or response promise is seeded. Production staff identity verification, custody, approval ownership and independent review remain operational prerequisites.
