# Independent human review

Case coordination requires current explicit service role and strong MFA. Source reporters/subjects cannot review their own case; source-domain independence is preserved. A pending outcome binds source snapshot, current author session and exact case revision with15-minute expiry. A different current staffed reviewer confirms it. Expired/changed evidence, revoked author, pending appeals or unfinished source blocks confirmation. Source outcomes/financial or clinical obligations are never rewritten by the coordinator.

Protected evidence, attribution and clinical records keep their existing purpose/review routes. Coordination approval grants no evidence access. Exceptional grants and especially consequential source enforcement are hardened in Q2; no universal break-glass is introduced in Q1. Independent appeals reuse L/M/P. Actual qualified staff/security-key custody and governance approval remain external.
