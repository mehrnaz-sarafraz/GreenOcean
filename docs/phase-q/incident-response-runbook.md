# Incident response runbook

Use the operations screen or authenticated `/api/v1/operations` API. Current explicit purpose, active account/session and strong MFA are required. ADMIN alone is insufficient. Staff must reference restricted evidence rather than paste personal, clinical or payment narratives. Follow [NIST SP800-61 Rev3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final) as a response reference, not a certification.

1. Detect/classify the actual failure. Record an opaque reference and retry key. Keep owner unassigned if genuine staffing is unavailable; do not promise immediate response.
2. Obtain an actual staffed owner's acknowledgement. Use the source-specific coverage workflow; route approval or a calendar entry alone does not establish presence.
3. Triage severity based on confidentiality, integrity, availability, obligations and recoverability, including serious single-subject impact. Review original evidence only through its existing authorized source workflow.
4. Propose narrow containment. A second independent current operator approves the exact incident revision and affected service. Listener queue/new admission, clinical booking/media, new payment initiation and educational publication controls run inside the owning source service. Account restriction also revokes sessions. No control deletes clinical records, refunds, invoices, financial events, existing messages or data-rights requests.
5. Investigate and retain action/reference history. Preserve ordinary own-data access, cancellation, reconciliation/refunds and category-specific rights; pre-existing privacy/eligibility gates still apply. Account reactivation uses its existing independent workflow, not a generic containment RESUME.
6. Propose recovery with current evidence. A different staffed operator approves recovery within fifteen minutes. Separately propose/approve release of this incident's hold; other incidents and runtime/region/processing approvals can still deny admission.
7. Resolve only after the incident's active holds have an appropriate approved recovery decision. Record post-incident review, lessons/follow-up references and closure. Statutory reporting duties and recipients require qualified human assessment; no universal deadline is invented.

Communications are fixed factual EN/FA templates: service interruption, recorded technical recovery, authenticated support. The owner drafts against the exact incident revision; an independent current staffed reviewer approves. Up to fifty distinct active affected accounts can receive existing SYSTEM_NOTICE inbox messages, or a minimized public service-status notice can be approved for up to24 hours. No private incident ID/evidence, allegation, patient identity or unestablished medical-breach claim is published. Inbox-created and marked-read counts are distinct from external delivery and actual human acknowledgement.

Default-off technical signal workers inspect only current metadata for repeated mail delivery failure, overdue unconfirmed refunds, waiting without qualified listener coverage and recent technical encounter failures. They deduplicate active same-kind/domain incidents, assign no invented owner and never auto-resolve. A failed database cannot reliably persist its own incident or alert; an independently operated external probe/receiver and actual outage drills are release prerequisites. Q4 adds controlled failure/recovery measurements; do not claim database-independent monitoring from this in-process worker.
