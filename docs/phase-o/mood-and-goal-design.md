# Mood observations and gentle goals
Mood labels are user-selected, non-clinical and not scored as good/bad. Owner history is bounded and editable with revisions; server timestamps govern observations. A timezone-aware date-range aggregate (maximum 366 days) describes only recorded counts. Missing days have no inferred value. No diagnostic, crisis, treatment or success inference is produced.
Goal steps are actual owner-managed milestones. Percentage is completed steps divided by current steps; an open-ended goal has self-reported status and no objective percentage. A goal supports active, paused, completed, reopened and archived status. Incomplete steps prevent declaring structured completion. Revision locks protect title/step/status edits; no shame, streak, popularity or sensitive-disclosure rewards exist. Legacy progress is preserved separately and never made verified completion. The former arbitrary-percentage PATCH operation returns a recoverable conflict directing callers to real steps.
