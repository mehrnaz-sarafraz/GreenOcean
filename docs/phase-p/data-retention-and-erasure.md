# Clinical and financial data lifecycle

The owner delegated technical retention handling. No legal duration was invented. `clinic_data_classes` defines fourteen separate classes, purposes, access, rights routes, backup treatment, incident handling and processor-transfer restrictions. All currently have UNAPPROVED retention and manual review. Automatic purge is disabled. Existing journal and earlier-phase retention decisions are unchanged.

The classes are professional identity, credentials, patient identity, booking, finance, clinical consent, clinical messages, clinical notes (including follow-up), clinical attachments, media metadata, reviews, ranking evidence, complaints and refund decisions. There is no shared blanket clinical/finance/journal expiry.

Authenticated subjects can request access, export, correction, erasure, restriction or appeal, optionally scoped to an own appointment. Stable retry keys and bounded intake prevent duplicate request creation. The clinic rights workspace shows immutable event history. An independent CLINIC_PRIVACY_OPERATOR with recent security-key authentication reviews the category and applicable exception. Evidence of an authorized action requires another operator's prior review. A current independently approved category hold prevents action-evidence closure. Holds are bounded and expire; renewal is a new auditable decision. This records accountable external action evidence, not a claim that this application automatically erased records or backups.

General account erasure returns a concrete rights-workflow route when governed care/finance exists. Direct clinical exports retain category, freshness and current grant restrictions; follow-up and feedback have purpose-isolated subject interfaces. Generic account archives carry clinic metadata and choices, with protected-content routes; clinical narratives are not copied into journal/social exports. Corrections preserve original record revisions. Unknown historical follow-up policies remain restricted.

Before production: obtain category-specific lawful processing/retention decisions, release approvals, independently reviewed notices, approved processors and locations, backup expiry/restore handling and an evidenced rights fulfillment procedure. Encrypted backup restore must reapply withdrawals, restrictions, holds and already authorized erasures before any new processing. No backup-erasure completion or provider transfer is asserted by synthetic QA.
