# Business Model Optimization handoff

Current immutable commercial policies configure commission basis points, exact provider fees and explicit refund terms per accepted booking. Administrative subscriptions allow configured periods/prices and allow-listed nonclinical entitlements. Renewal requires explicit acceptance; no recurring debit exists. Free appointments remain free. Settlement, promotion, clinical qualification, education and reputation remain separately authorized.

A later business-model workstream may evaluate provider economics, commission percentages, administrative tier packaging, organization plans, referral channels, discounts and separately lawful labelled sponsored placements. No permanent pricing, margin forecast, clinical upselling, unnecessary appointments, outcome marketing or patient-data sales are introduced. Any new commercial version must preserve existing accepted contracts and exact integer currency accounting.
