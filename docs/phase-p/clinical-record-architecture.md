# Isolated clinical records

V76–V78 add consent, relationship, encounter, transport participation, encrypted message/note/attachment, access-request/grant and immutable access-audit tables. Existing clinical-looking legacy labels are not imported. No social, peer, mood, journal or education data is linked automatically. Clinical encryption uses `greenocean.clinic.record-keys` and `record-current-key`, distinct from credential configuration, with authenticated purpose/record/owner context and key IDs for rotation. Missing keys fail closed. Backup operators need separately approved encrypted snapshots, protected key custody and restore procedures; a successful synthetic restore does not establish production infrastructure assurance.

Assigned professionals need current qualifications, scope, strong authentication, current consent and the approved time window. Patient direct access depends on both the original and current category policy. Shared draft notes are not returned as finalized patient records. Professional-only notes require an explicit category decision; a rights review is supported rather than asserting universal access or exemption. Final notes receive append-only amendments; concurrent revisions cannot overwrite each other.

ADMIN, finance, community moderator, listener and editor roles provide no general narrative access. A current CLINICAL_RECORDS_OPERATOR needs recent MFA and a separate independently approved purpose/category/encounter grant expiring within24 hours. The requestor cannot approve their own grant; revocation/expiry/current approver role are checked on each read. Narrative-free audit records identify access, resource and exceptional grant.

Clinical exports are separate from the generic account archive. Patient password reconfirmation, current category policy and authenticated page cursors are required. Message pages contain at most50 items; note history pages at most200. Export identifies continuation and categories requiring review; protected attachments use individual audited downloads. No universal deletion period is invented. Pending category-specific legal/clinical/financial decisions restrict production and automatic erasure; the owner delegated technical handling, not fabricated legal evidence. P5 supplies case management and lifecycle reporting.

Clinical service integration also distinguishes agreed financial snapshots from earned payables. A refund before delivery cannot reverse an unearned commission; a refund after evidenced delivery compensates only existing earned entries. Finance views expose captured confirmation time, earned amounts, refund reversals, net due and holds separately.
