# P2 booking and financial operations

P2 extends `consultation_requests` and existing professional slots. It does not rebuild listener scheduling or turn historical completed requests into clinical delivery or financial evidence. V74–V75 preserve legacy rows and add separately governed appointments, immutable commercial versions, invoices, provider evidence, an append-only event ledger, refund obligations, disputes, subscription orders and settlement requests.

The patient must explicitly accept the exact slot revision, price and policy. The professional must accept the immutable commercial policy for that slot. Direct free reservations become CONFIRMED; exceptional requests require professional acceptance; paid reservations require independently reconciled exact provider capture. A configurable professional fee is stored in integer IRR; ten IRR is one toman for display only. Commission basis points, provider fee and terms are frozen at booking. No tax, discount, partial refund or commercial price is invented. Free slots have zero fees. User and professional schedule locks plus database exclusion/unique constraints protect independent connections. Cancelled slots remain cancelled; professionals publish fresh capacity explicitly.

Every retry uses durable operation identity. Payment intent identity equals appointment UUID; subscription intent identity equals the accepted retry UUID; settlement identity equals appointment UUID; refunds use the persisted obligation UUID. Adapters must honor these keys even if the server loses a response before commit. Provider references and event keys are independently checked for reference, operation, currency and exact amount. Browser return URLs cannot confirm a payment. No real adapter is installed; deterministic fake evidence exists only in test source. The provider contract supports creation, reconciliation, refunds, subscription billing and settlement. A selected provider needs its own authenticated callback verification, timeout/recovery/reconciliation conformance tests and independent owner authorization before production. No card credentials are accepted or stored.

Captured late payments create refund obligations instead of reviving expired appointments. Professional cancellation after capture creates a full obligation. Patient cancellation uses independent dispute/refund review because no automatic patient-cancellation penalty policy is approved. Repeated cancellation, capture and refund reconciliation cannot duplicate immutable ledger kinds. A liability is not a wallet. Refund processing becomes REFUND_CONFIRMED only after independently matching provider evidence. The 48-hour initiation objective is distinct from banking availability. The default-off worker requires an explicitly configured currently authorized financial operator and provider approvals; uncertain failures become MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Operations show overdue obligations. Ten-minute professional no-show review requires elapsed server time, a confirmed appointment, explicit operational reconciliation and no contradictory server readiness/service evidence. Missing browser heartbeat alone cannot issue a refund decision. P5 strengthens operational incident linkage.

Settlement requires completed/captured service, actual earned payable evidence, no open dispute or refund, an approved banking arrangement and provider confirmation. P3 supplies service-completion evidence; P2 cannot manufacture it. A provider-acknowledged request is not a settled payout. Subscriptions have explicitly configured version/price/period and allow-listed administrative entitlements. They grant no clinical authority, editorial permission or quality ranking, and do not auto-renew. Paid subscriptions require provider evidence. No plans are seeded.

Patient `/clinic` workspace and professional earnings show invoices, charge state, provider references and obligations separately. `/clinic-finance-operations` requires current ADMIN or CLINIC_FINANCE authority plus recent security-key authentication. Community moderators cannot access financial operations. Clinical narratives are absent. Professional workspace exposes agreement and subscription selection; its consent does not replace patient clinical consent.

Production `greenocean.clinic.commerce-enabled` is false by default. Financial/clinical retention and legal processing remain unapproved. Their presence prevents the generic account eraser from cascading through audit records; the separate privacy-rights workflow is required, with explicit category decisions rather than a claimed blanket retention exception. Existing account deletion for accounts without governed clinical/financial data remains available. Own financial metadata is included in the authenticated account archive. No real license, payment, payout or legal approval was exercised by tests.
