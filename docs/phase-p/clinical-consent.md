# Separate clinical consent

No public clinical notice is seeded or inferred from community acceptance. An administrator authors an exact kind/version/locale/mode notice; a different current administrator reviews that exact revision. Published wording is immutable. Platform terms, care consent and clinical data notice are separate; audio/video also requires media disclosure. Expired or superseded wording cannot authorize new processing. Patient choices are append-only events bound to the appointment and exact notice; retries cannot change meaning. Withdrawal stops active entry/processing and remains in history. It does not invent a legal right to erase every retained record.

The clinical workspace displays full wording and individual choices in English or Persian/RTL. A separate currently authorized record-category policy is required for records. Production record processing is off by default; synthetic notice/release entries used by tests are not legal approval.
