# Weekly recognition

Profession and consultation mode define cohorts. Categories are reliable appointments, reviewed educational contribution and newcomer discovery. Newcomers receive discovery without invented scores. Current qualification, platform promotion permission, available offers, independent method approval, holds, fair rotation and a bounded group-wide weekly limit apply. Paid plans cannot buy inclusion.

A proposal records an immutable public-input digest, methodology and selection basis. A different current authorized reviewer must publish it. A changed input or methodology requires a fresh proposal. Public output distinguishes original selection basis from current evidence and rechecks eligibility. Withdrawal and reasons are audited; concurrent publishing cannot exceed the group/week bound.

A feature is an editorial service-discovery selection, never an assertion of best clinician or superior outcomes. Open complaints alone do not reduce rankings; independently decided feature holds restrict weekly recognition without secretly changing experience scores.
