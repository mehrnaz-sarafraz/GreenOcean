# Current authorization
Every clinical offer and care access rechecks exact independently verified application evidence, current reviewer role, account status, validity, recheck deadline, country/modality and current platform approvals. Historic VERIFIED labels confer no authority. Forward V68 binds scopes to that exact application and fixes the initial parameter collision; V71 also preserves the earlier independently reviewed care-region expiry. Revocation cancels unreserved drafts/offers, removes readiness and denies new access; reserved appointments are preserved for explicit cancellation/refund handling. Decisions and evidence snapshots are append-only through the application service. Reviewer account erasure revokes their platform approvals and removes identifying audit references; current-role checks fail closed. Worker-driven external registry revalidation is not configured; an expired recheck deadline blocks care until a real independent decision.
