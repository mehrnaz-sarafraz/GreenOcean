# Voluntary follow-up and operational recovery

Only the assigned currently qualified professional, with recent security-key authentication and current clinical consent/access, can author a versioned encrypted recommendation after evidenced service completion. Each revision binds its exact category policy. Only its patient accepts, declines or withdraws. New revisions require a fresh choice. Recommendation validity is bounded; reminder time is optional and selected by the patient. There are no AI plans, automatic appointments, inherited access for a new clinician or ongoing message permission.

Repeat/replacement booking reuses exact offered price, policy, revision and explicit acceptance. Cancellation and replacement occur in one transaction, with ordered professional locks and a patient lock. A failed replacement rolls back cancellation. Retry returns the same replacement. Original charges and refund disputes remain on the original appointment; funds are never silently moved. Active encounters must finish before recovery booking.

Operational dashboards separate professional schedule/qualification, finance reconciliation/overdue liabilities, safety incidents and credential restrictions, privacy requests/holds, and aggregate business reporting. Operational roles do not grant access to clinical text. Business queries never join narrative tables. Administrative subscriptions have explicit ACTIVE_NO_AUTO_RENEWAL / EXPIRED_EXPLICIT_RENEWAL_REQUIRED states; a different retry key cannot purchase an overlapping active/pending subscription.

Incident intake accepts fixed categories and no clinical narrative. Two distinct current operations staff independently author and approve a classification against a bounded source reference. The participants/reporting actor cannot approve their own incident. Finance no-show review additionally requires elapsed configurable grace (default ten minutes, bounded five to sixty), server-observed patient check-in, independent no-outage review and absence of contradictory readiness/service/outage evidence. A checkbox or missing heartbeat alone cannot establish professional blame. Unresolved/technical evidence sends the case to individual review.

Refund and payout receipts remain provider-confirmed. Overdue initiation, pending/failed payment/refund and processing settlement/refund holds are surfaced. A confirmed refund after settlement exposes recovery_review_units; it does not silently manufacture a new debit or negative spendable balance. The production provider, refund worker and clinical flags remain off until actual approval.

State changes create low-disclosure authenticated in-app notices. Follow-up reminders require explicit global opt-in and the latest accepted per-version choice, current consent/eligibility, an unexpired recommendation, timezone-aware quiet hours and deduplication. Equal quiet-hour endpoints mean all-day silence. There is no push-receipt or physical attendance claim. Native push delivery requires a separate approved adapter/device validation.

Offer discovery checks each offered modality independently; an approved audio/video-only professional is not incorrectly required to hold text authorization. Exact booking and activation still recheck that offer’s actual modality.
